---
description: Takings by practitioner - visits and dollars over any window, off completed visits, beside the rebooking rate that decides whether next month looks like this one.
---

1. Run `node scripts/clinic.mjs takings --json` (add `--days=N`; 7 is the default) and `rebooking --json`.
2. Present takings per practitioner with visit counts, then the rebooking rate beside it: a full week with a low rebooking rate is a quiet month coming.
3. If asked for trend, run two windows (`--days=7` and `--days=14`) and compare halves.
